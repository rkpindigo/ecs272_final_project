## Visualization Ideas

- **Bar → Line Interaction**: bar chart for overview, click to show detailed line chart (time series)
- **Spider/Radar Chart**: visual appeal for race‑category breakdown, but may be hard to read

- **Legend Hover Highlight**: hover over race or gender in legend to emphasize corresponding polygon in radar chart
- **Gradient Table**: use color gradients to represent percentages of winners by race/gender instead of bars
