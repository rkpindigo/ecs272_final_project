Notes (Additive, Non-Intrusive Suggestions):
- The age-based thread is interesting, but our current age data seems to be winners-only and ends around 2005. If we keep age as a chapter, we may want to label it as “historical context” rather than “past 10 years.”
- For “progress,” we could compare nominee share vs winner share (e.g., Female and Non-White). This helps show whether representation in nominations translates into wins.
- Category grouping could make the story clearer (Acting, Directing, Writing, Technical, Music, Picture/Production). This can reduce noise from the long list of categories.
- The race spider/radar chart is compelling but may need a second view (e.g., heatmap or stacked area) to make subtle changes easier to see.
- The “Who benefits from the progress?” matrix idea feels strong. We could frame it as pre-2015 vs post-2015 (or pre/post #OscarsSoWhite) and show winners vs nominees as a toggle.
